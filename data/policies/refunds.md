# Refunds

## Refund timeline
Refunds are sent to your original payment method. After we receive and inspect a returned item, the refund is approved within 2 business days. Your bank or card issuer then takes 5 to 7 business days to show the money. Refunds for cancelled orders usually take 5 to 7 business days in total.

## Refund methods
We refund to the original payment method only. We cannot refund to a different card, bank account, or as cash. If you paid with store credit, the refund is returned as store credit instantly.

## Partial refunds
We may issue a partial refund if an item is returned with missing accessories, signs of use, or damaged packaging that was not caused by shipping. The return shipping fee for change-of-mind returns is also deducted. You will be emailed the reason for any deduction.

## Refund not received
If it has been more than 10 business days since your refund was approved and you have not received it, first check with your bank. If the money is still missing, contact support and we will open an investigation with our payment provider.

## Refund status
You can check refund status any time in your account or by asking support with your order ID. Statuses are initiated, processing, completed, or rejected.

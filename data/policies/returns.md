# Returns and Exchanges

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The return clock starts on the day the carrier marks the package as delivered. Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but may still be covered by the warranty.

## Item condition
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories. Opened earbuds and headphones can only be returned if they are defective, for hygiene reasons. Gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Open the order in your account and select Return item, or contact support with your order ID. We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Pack the item securely and drop it with the carrier named on the label.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is free if the item is defective, damaged, or we sent the wrong item. For change-of-mind returns, a flat return shipping fee of 4.99 USD is deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want. This is the fastest way to make sure the replacement is in stock.

## Damaged or defective items
If your item arrives damaged or stops working, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order ID and a photo of the problem. We will arrange a free return and send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

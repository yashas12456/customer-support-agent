# Warranty and Repairs

## Warranty period
All electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. Accessories such as cables and chargers carry a 6-month warranty. Outdoor and fitness products carry a 90-day warranty.

## What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage beyond the product's stated rating, battery wear after 12 months, or damage from unauthorised repairs.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your order ID, a short description of the fault, and a photo or video. If the claim is approved we send a prepaid label and either repair or replace the product within 10 business days of receiving it.

## Replacement or refund
If a product cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same model or an equivalent. If no equivalent is available, we refund the purchase price.

## Escalation
Safety issues such as overheating, smoke, or electric shock are treated as urgent. Stop using the product immediately and contact support; a specialist will follow up.

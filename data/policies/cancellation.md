# Order Cancellation

## When you can cancel
You can cancel an order only while its status is Processing, which means it has not been handed to the carrier. Once an order is Shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

## Cancelling a shipped order
If your order has already shipped, you cannot cancel it. You can refuse delivery or, after it arrives, return it under our returns policy within 30 days of delivery.

## Refund after cancellation
When an order is cancelled, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. No cancellation fee applies.

## Changing an order
We cannot edit the items or address of an order after it is placed. To change an order, cancel it while it is Processing and place a new one.

## Pre-orders and custom items
Pre-orders can be cancelled any time before the dispatch date. Personalised items cannot be cancelled once production begins.

# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of being placed, excluding weekends and public holidays.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of 50 USD or more, and 5.99 USD below that. Express shipping costs 12.99 USD on all orders.

## Tracking
Once your order ships you receive an email with the carrier name and tracking number. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delayed or lost packages
If your package has not arrived 3 business days after the expected delivery date, contact support. We will open a carrier investigation, and if the package is confirmed lost we will send a replacement or a full refund.

## International shipping
We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and India. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged on delivery.

## Wrong address
We cannot change the delivery address after an order has been placed. If the package has not shipped, cancel and reorder. If it has shipped, contact the carrier with your tracking number to request redirection.
